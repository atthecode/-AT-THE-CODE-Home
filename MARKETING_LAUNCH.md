## Conversion rules
- Send all paid traffic to /sourcing.html, not the generic homepage.
- Lead with the problem solved, not the company story.
- Keep £9.99 visible.
- State that it is a sourcing/research service, not the price of furniture.
- Use one clear CTA: Start my search.
- Do not run paid ads until Stripe customer-facing branding is aligned with AT THE CODE Home / AT THE CODE.

## After first 5 paid customers
Review completion time and customer feedback. If delivery quality is strong, test £14.99. If conversion remains healthy, test £19.99 with an enhanced option including 5–8 choices or a room bundle.
