# AT THE CODE Home — Supplier Audit

Internal owner file. Do not publish supplier names, trade prices or private terms to customers.

## Approval rule
A supplier only moves to **Approved for launch** after all of the following are confirmed in writing:
1. UK company/trade identity and current trading status.
2. Dropship/direct-to-customer model with no speculative stock purchase.
3. Any deposit, opening balance, membership fee or minimum-order rule.
4. Trade price/margin and VAT treatment.
5. Live stock/feed or a reliable stock-confirmation process.
6. UK delivery area, lead times and surcharges.
7. Damage, missing-part, cancellation and returns process.
8. Neutral/white-label packaging where required.
9. Permission to use supplier product images/videos/descriptions.
10. Relevant UK product/safety/compliance documents.
11. Review-pattern check for repeated delivery/refund/quality complaints.

## Launch principle
AT THE CODE Home should behave as a curated department store, not an open marketplace. Fewer reliable suppliers are preferable to a huge catalogue with poor fulfilment.
