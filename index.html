How AT THE CODE Home works.

We use a supplier-direct model so we can curate more choice without buying rooms full of stock before customers want it.

1

We source

We look for established UK trade suppliers and products with practical delivery routes.

2

We verify

We check supplier terms, price rules, delivery, returns, product information and media permission.

3

You order

Once a verified product is live, you order through AT THE CODE Home at the displayed price.

4

Partner fulfils

Our approved fulfilment partner delivers the order to the customer under the confirmed delivery terms.

The AT THE CODE check

Not just a cheap price on a screen.

Before a product is switched on for sale, we want the details that matter after checkout: the real price, who delivers it, what happens if it arrives damaged, and whether the supplier can support the sale properly.

Before launch

What customers should know.

Can I buy products today?

The store is in its supplier-onboarding stage. Checkout will be enabled only for products that have completed the launch checks.

Will you sell only sofas?

No. The opening plan covers living room, bedroom, storage, lighting, dining and selected kitchen/home appliances.

Will delivery be free?

Delivery will be stated on each live listing. We will not describe delivery as free unless the supplier terms confirm it for that product and destination.

What about electrical items?

Electrical lighting and appliances will launch later than non-electrical home items while we complete the extra UK product, warranty and recycling checks.
