How AT THE CODE Home works.

The shop is being built around a dropship-first model so stock is not purchased speculatively before customer demand exists.

1

We source

Potential products are researched and supplier details are checked before launch.

2

We verify

We confirm what the advertised price includes and check UK delivery terms.

3

You order

Once a product is live, customers order through AT THE CODE Home.

4

Supplier ships

The approved supplier fulfils the order directly to the customer.

They are sourcing categories, not confirmed products. Only verified items will become purchasable listings.

Will delivery be free?

Delivery will be shown on each live product listing. We won’t advertise free delivery unless it has been confirmed for that item and destination.
