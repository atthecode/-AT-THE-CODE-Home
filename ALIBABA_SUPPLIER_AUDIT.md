# AT THE CODE Home — Alibaba Supplier Audit

Internal owner file. Alibaba suppliers are not approved for launch solely because they show MOQ 1, Trade Assurance or a high platform rating.

## Alibaba approval rule
A supplier only moves beyond research after all of the following are confirmed in writing:
1. Verified Supplier / manufacturer status and trading history.
2. MOQ 1 or true per-order dropshipping for the exact SKU.
3. UK DDP door-to-door quotation for a real customer postcode.
4. No hidden customs, VAT, port, handling or residential-delivery fees.
5. Trade Assurance order route and payment kept inside Alibaba.
6. Exact damage/refund/replacement process for single-customer orders.
7. Product testing/certification relevant to the UK product category.
8. Neutral packaging and no supplier invoices/marketing to the customer.
9. Written permission to use/edit product photos and videos.
10. Sample/pilot order before scale for bulky or electrical items.

## Alibaba categories that look most useful for AT THE CODE Home
1. Vacuum/compressed sofas — lower-volume shipping than conventional sofas, but UK fire-compliance documentation is mandatory before listing.
2. Rugs/mats — lower damage risk and simpler UK compliance than bulky furniture.
3. Decorative home accessories — good fit where MOQ 1 and DDP are available.
4. Garden furniture — possible, but bulky-item DDP and damage claims must be proven.
5. Lighting — potentially attractive margins, but electrical/WEEE/UKCA checks make this a later-stage category.

## Non-negotiable Alibaba rules
- Never pay a supplier outside Alibaba for the first orders.
- Never rely on a product-page freight estimate alone; obtain a written DDP quote to a real UK postcode.
- Never list upholstered furniture without UK fire-safety evidence for the exact model/material combination.
- Never list electrical goods based only on CE/UKCA icons on a page; obtain the declaration/test evidence for the exact SKU.
- Start with one pilot order before scaling any bulky Alibaba supplier.
- Keep UK wholesalers as the core catalogue; use Alibaba selectively where it gives a genuinely better product or margin without creating customer-service risk.
